Too Modern

November 20, 2008 · All Posts, Asides

At seventeen years of age I was refused entry into a church. I asked: "Why?" Their response: "Your hair is too long." My reply: "Didn't Jesus have long hair?" Their reply: "Yeah, but yours is too modern." True story.

Outside of weddings or funerals, that was the last time I went to a church on my own.
