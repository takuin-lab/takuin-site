Your Idea of Enlightenment

October 15, 2008 · All Posts, Asides

The idea, "Enlightenment is this," or, "Liberation is that," might make you feel significant or give you solidity and security, but what is it good for? It is only your best guess. Where will your ideas of enlightenment take you? Is it possible for you to cast aside all these ideas you've been handed?

Eric,

It is very interesting to investigate all of this. I always like to ask, "Does the idealized state create the words, or do the words create the idealized state?" Although I would not place too much importance on the words as a device for personal gain.

If one lived on an island, and had never heard the word enlightenment, does that mean he or she would be doomed to suffer the indignities of their own mind? Because this person has no outside system to help them, will they be forever greedy and selfish? Or is there a something, beyond the systems and concepts we build, that might bring an end to all of it?

It is easy to say YES, but if one is not there, it is merely a concept. And if one IS there, and they say I am there (or alternately, I Am That), they have gone nowhere.

Just sit sit sit and listen. It will come. But it isn't an answer such as, This plus This = That. Not something we can reference from what has come before.

It may just become clear. Suddenly. And there is nothing one can do to prepare for it. But that is the beauty of this life, within these limited bodies. Even in death there is always a new beginning.

I look forward to the time I will be on foot.
