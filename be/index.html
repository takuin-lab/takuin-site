Be

May 6, 2008 · All Posts, Quick Thoughts

The light pours in,
eyes will open,
but consciousness
will not
collapse
to
a
point.

Energy seeps
through the borders
of body,
dissolving the
phenomenal
into
the spiritual.

One with all things,
All things being one.

"You are the wind.
Go and breathe life
into this world."

Be.
