Uzma,

What I meant to say, are the thoughts rushing through you, or are you the very thoughts that are rushing? Is it possible to be lost in them? Follow what is happening. I am not saying it is a certain thing that you will be able to fully hold and own. It is nothing like that at all. But is separation part of the problem?

Be fully alive in all of these moments. Is there some resistance happening? Do the thoughts rush and then someone stands up and says, "No, that is not right!" If they rush, they rush. That becomes your meditation. Meditation is never something you should do, or a technique that will bring results later. Meditation is this living with what is, as it is.

Thoughts rush, certainly. But is there also someone rushing with them?

I am not saying it is so, one way or the other. Sit with yourself and find out.
