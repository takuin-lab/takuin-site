No Mystery

September 20, 2008 · All Posts, Asides

None of this is a mystery. The way the body functions from moment to moment, the thoughts that may or may not arise, the hair on your head falling into your face. Always as it is; never as it should be.

The only mystery is the one you have created. Outside of your ideas, where does it exist?
