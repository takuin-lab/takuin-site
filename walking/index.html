Walking

November 17, 2008 · All Posts, Asides, The Beautiful Earth

There is something beautiful in walking. It clears the mind and opens the senses. Walking through nature, and listening to the movements of this beautiful earth, reveals all you could ever ask for. But you must listen. Hold your head up, move with that natural rhythm, and listen.

If there is a question, or an answer, it is all revealed in nature.
