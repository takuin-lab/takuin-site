What Arrives, Soon

I now notice a quiet apparatus assembling itself: notes, scraps, and brief signals. Most of it stays, now, as much as anything does. If this is treason against impermanence, make the most of it.

A WORD ABOUT THIS CONSTRUCTION

1) Only modest materials are used: plain text, a little light, the sound of a page turning. The aim is clarity without ornament, ornament without noise.

2) Posts appear, unannounced, and stay. If a piece matters to you, it will still be here later — but sit with it now anyway. The archive is the breath you hold.

3) The green-titled entries may hide a door. Some lead to quiet rooms, some to maps, and occasionally to nothing at all.

4) This site accepts messages, plainly, in the comments below each piece. Words only, for now.
