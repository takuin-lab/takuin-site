Tom Stine's Online Satsang

June 17, 2010 · All Posts, Odds and Ends, Quick Thoughts

This is just a quick note to remind everyone that my friend Tom Stine is holding a live online satsang on June 20th. The details are available on Tom's site here: Live Satsang Online.

For those of you unfamiliar with this concept of satsang, Tom explains it well:

If you've never been to a satsang before, let me give you a brief overview of what it is and how I plan to do it. The word satsang means "coming together to talk about the truth." Pretty straightforward. The format will be pretty straightforward, too: I will talk for about 20-45 minutes, and then I will answer questions and dialogue with anyone who wishes to speak with me.


It is sure to be a fine experience for those attending, and this will be Tom's first satsang, so head over there and show him your support. I was planning on attending, but I will be in the mountains again for four days and won't return until the 21st.

I'm happy Tom was able to finally get this off the ground, as I think he's been planning something like this for some time.

Break a leg, Tom. ;)
