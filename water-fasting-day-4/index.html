Felipe,

If you read the other posts in this series, you will understand what I mean with the calories.

This was a mental experiment, and not a cleanse. I did not care at all about ketosis. The entire purpose was to find out what goes on in the mind during a prolonged fast. While there may have been cleansing and ketosis going on, that was not at all the point of doing this fast.

It ended up being not a strict 'water' fast. By the end of the month, I was taking in 500 calories or less in other liquids. I found that I could not take vitamin and mineral supplements while on this fast; it just made me feel queasy. So instead, I would make a drink containing said vitamins and minerals, and that is where the calories came from.

This was an experiment in finding out. Not an attempt to cleanse or lose weight.
