November Project: Question #5

A quick statement, a quick response...

I would think that being 'In the NOW' or 'Living in the Moment' would be a step below that of complete liberation.

A step below liberation is thinking you need to take steps toward liberation. 'Steps' or 'Becoming' implies a process involving time. Liberation requires no time at all.

Wanting to become pulls you away from what you already are.
