Just Listen

November 1, 2008 · All Posts, Asides

You'll sit with a thoughtful posture, perhaps gently tapping your fingers together. They'll pour out their hearts, but suddenly, the center will arise. It will tell you what is right and what is wrong. It will provide your reply, and give you the superiority to believe you are right. It will do everything but allow you to listen.
