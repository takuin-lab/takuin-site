New Site is Finally Here

January 14, 2008 · All Posts

After hours of staring at the computer monitor, trying to make sense of CSS and .php, the new site is finally finished.

(If you are reading this on my RSS, please click through and check out the new design.)

The new categories at the right are not completely linked yet. Only the Question of the Week, and Quick Thoughts are linked, as they are the only categories with content (at this writing). But as I go along this week, I will be filling all of that in.

The only thing I would ask of all of you is to report any bugs, or any strange things you might come across. Also, if you have any ideas or comments on the design, please let me know. Just reply to this post, or pop in the Contact area.

Also, there are a few new pages that I have not uploaded just yet. A new About page, a page about Realization, and a guide to this site as well as what the various ongoing series are about.

I didn't want to wait to go live with the new site, so as I write these new pages and fill in the various new categories, I'll be sure to mention it in an aside at the end of a regular post.

I hope you all had a safe and wonderful New Year, and I look forward to interacting with all of you even more in 2008.

Takuin
